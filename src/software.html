Software

ASSIST
Ephemeris-quality integrations of test particles in the solar system, built on REBOUND. Used for orbit fitting and prediction of asteroids and comets.
REBOUND (WHFast)
The Wisdom–Holman map is the basis of REBOUND's WHFast integrator.
HelioLinC
Linking of minor-planet detections across nights in sparse survey data, now part of Rubin Observatory's solar system pipeline.
